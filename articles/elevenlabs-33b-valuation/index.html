ElevenLabs in Early Talks for a $33 Billion Valuation: What We Know

November 26, 2026

ElevenLabs, the AI voice startup, is in early talks for a new funding round that would value it at about $33 billion, according to Bloomberg, which published the report on November 25, 2026. The round is reportedly aimed at early 2027.

What is reported

  • The talks are early, so terms and investors can change.
  • $33 billion is the valuation being targeted, not a closed deal.
  • The Next Web and Investing.com carried the same Bloomberg-based report.

What is not confirmed

  • Neither the company nor any investor has announced a round.
  • The size of the raise and the investors are not confirmed in the reports we reviewed.

Why it matters

A valuation at this level would put ElevenLabs among the most valuable AI startups focused on voice. It also shows investors still pay high prices for fast-growing AI companies.

We will update this page if the company or its investors confirm anything.
